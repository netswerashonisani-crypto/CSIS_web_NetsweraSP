Welcome to the Dark Side of Romance

Dark romance is a genre that explores relationships through intense, complicated and sometimes disturbing situations. These stories often contain morally grey characters, dangerous relationships, secrets, obsession, manipulation, revenge and forbidden attraction. Unlike traditional romance stories, dark romance allows readers to explore the complicated side of love and the choices people make when their emotions become difficult to control.

Our collection is created for readers who enjoy stories that are unpredictable, emotional and mysterious. Every book offers a different experience, from dangerous enemies who become lovers to characters trapped between desire and fear. If you enjoy stories where nothing is completely innocent and love can become both beautiful and dangerous, you may find something here for you.

Obsession

Stories where attraction becomes intense and characters struggle to separate love from obsession.

Forbidden Love

Relationships that are difficult, dangerous or forbidden because of family, society, secrets or circumstances.

Stories involving characters surrounded by danger, violence, crime or powerful enemies.

Explore Our Dark Romance Collection

Our book collection is designed for readers who enjoy romance with a darker and more intense atmosphere. These stories explore complicated relationships, forbidden attraction, obsession, secrets, betrayal, power and emotional conflict. Each book takes readers into a different world where love is not always simple and the characters often have complicated personalities and difficult pasts. Explore the collection below and discover a story that matches your reading interests.

Haunting Adeline

H.D. Carlton

Haunting Adeline follows Adeline, a young woman who becomes the focus of a mysterious and obsessive man. After moving into her grandmother's old home, Adeline begins discovering secrets connected to the house and its history. At the same time, she realizes that someone has been watching her from the shadows. The story combines romance, mystery, psychological tension and danger, creating an atmosphere where attraction and fear exist side by side. Readers who enjoy dark settings, mysterious characters, secrets and intense relationships may find this story particularly interesting.

Themes: Obsession, mystery, danger, psychological tension, secrets and dark romance.

Punk 57

Penelope Douglas

Punk 57 tells the story of two teenagers who have built a close friendship through letters without meeting each other in person. They believe they understand one another, but everything changes when they finally discover each other's real identities. Their relationship becomes complicated as anger, attraction, secrets and misunderstandings begin to surface. The story explores how people can create different versions of themselves depending on who they are with and how difficult it can be to separate friendship, attraction and resentment.

Themes: Identity, friendship, attraction, secrets, emotional conflict and complicated relationships.

God of Malice

Rina Kent

God of Malice introduces readers to a dangerous and intense relationship between characters who are drawn to each other despite the circumstances surrounding them. The story focuses on obsession, attraction, control and power while exploring characters who do not fit neatly into the categories of hero or villain. As the relationship develops, the characters are forced to deal with their own desires, fears and secrets. The dark atmosphere and morally grey characters make the story suitable for readers who enjoy romance with a strong sense of danger and emotional tension.

Themes: Obsession, power, attraction, control, danger and morally grey characters.

The Sweetest Oblivion

Danielle Lori

The Sweetest Oblivion takes readers into the world of powerful families, loyalty and mafia traditions. The story follows a woman whose life is influenced by family expectations and the dangerous world surrounding her. Her relationship with a powerful and mysterious man creates a conflict between what she is expected to do and what she actually wants. The story combines romance with family pressure, forbidden attraction and the risks that come with becoming involved with people who live by their own rules.

Themes: Mafia, forbidden love, family, loyalty, power, attraction and secrets.

Credence

Penelope Douglas

Credence follows a young woman who leaves her familiar life behind and moves to an isolated location where she must adjust to a completely different environment. Away from the people and routines she once knew, she becomes caught in complicated relationships that challenge her understanding of herself and the people around her. The isolated setting creates an intense atmosphere while the characters deal with attraction, boundaries, emotions and difficult personal choices.

Themes: Isolation, attraction, family, emotional conflict, boundaries and complicated relationships.

Twisted Love

Ana Huang

Twisted Love focuses on a relationship that begins with complicated circumstances and develops into something neither character expected. Alex is known for being cold, controlled and emotionally distant, while Ava brings warmth and emotion into his carefully controlled world. As they become closer, secrets from the past begin to affect their relationship. The story explores attraction, trust, protection, revenge and the difficulty of allowing another person to see the parts of yourself that you normally keep hidden.

Themes: Secrets, protection, revenge, attraction, trust and emotional conflict.

The Maddest Obsession

Danielle Lori

The Maddest Obsession explores a complicated relationship between two people whose personalities and circumstances make their connection difficult to ignore. Their lives repeatedly cross as attraction, tension and emotional conflict continue to grow. The story combines romance with the dangerous world surrounding powerful characters and explores how obsession can remain even when people try to move on. It is a story of tension, attraction, personal struggles and relationships that refuse to remain simple.

Themes: Obsession, attraction, mafia, emotional tension, power and complicated relationships.

King of Wrath

Ana Huang

King of Wrath centres on a relationship created through circumstances that neither character originally chose for themselves. Dante is powerful, controlled and focused on his goals, while Vivian is unexpectedly pulled into his world. As they spend more time together, the relationship develops beyond the arrangement that brought them together. The story explores power, family expectations, secrets, attraction and the gradual development of feelings between two very different people.

Themes: Power, family expectations, secrets, attraction, trust and arranged relationships.
