What is Dark Romance?

Dark romance is a subgenre of romance fiction that explores heavier themes — morally grey or villainous heroes, power imbalances, emotional intensity, and uncomfortable premises — while still delivering a happily ever after. These stories pluge readers into a gritty realities of crime,mystery ,capativity ,revenge ,pushing the very boundariers of love and obsession.
